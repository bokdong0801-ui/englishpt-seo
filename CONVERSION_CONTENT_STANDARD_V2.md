# CONVERSION CONTENT STANDARD V2

기준일: 2026-09-18
상태: production hard gate

## 1. Conversion objective
Every page must move the reader through:
**identify → understand → believe the method → judge fit → act**

Information density alone is not enough.

## 2. Three-page-zone rule
### Top ~25%
Goal: “This is my problem.”
Required:
- keyword-intent clarity
- diagnosis snapshot
- decision/trust strip
- self-identification or immediate pain
- clear scope/boundary

### Middle ~50%
Goal: “I understand why this method is different.”
Required:
- actual problem mechanics
- WHY/CHECK/TRAIN/RECHECK
- diagnosis dimensions
- training flow
- feedback/recheck logic
- illustrative scenes

### Bottom ~25%
Goal: “I can decide what to do next.”
Required:
- fit / other path
- FAQ
- deep guides that add new decision information
- internal-link map
- final consultation CTA

## 3. Problem micro-story contract
A major problem block must answer:
1. WHY — why it happens
2. CHECK — what is observed
3. TRAIN — what is practiced
4. RECHECK — what changes are checked next time

A generic statement such as "customized training is provided" does not count.

## 4. Readability
- one judgment question per block
- short paragraphs
- visible subheadings
- every 2–3 text blocks should be followed by a visual/structural break
- avoid long uninterrupted walls of text
- CTA should appear only after enough value has been established

## 5. Audience specificity
A page fails if another audience name can be substituted without rewriting the core.
Examples:
- high: performance presentation, follow-up, summary, school rubric, speaking-to-interview bridge
- middle: school exam/performance/grammar/reading/listening management
- elementary: foundation/reading/vocab/routine/parent judgment
- university: campus/presentation/seminar/exchange/deadlines
- job: STAR/motivation/follow-up/interview/recruiting timeline
- worker: meetings/presentations/proposals/negotiation/stakeholders
- homemaker: restart/daily/travel/sustainable routine

## 6. Scope clarity
The page must explicitly state what it does NOT primarily solve.
Example for high-conv:
- written internal-exam grammar → other path
- CSAT reading speed → other path
- performance speaking / follow-up / oral summary → core

This reduces wrong-fit leads and improves conversion quality.

## 7. Proof and honesty
Forbidden without evidence:
- fabricated reviews
- fabricated score gains
- guaranteed results
- invented student quotes
- fixed time-to-result claims

Allowed:
- illustrative scenarios clearly labeled
- sample feedback reports clearly labeled
- observable behavior and process
- verified user-provided testimonials when available

## 8. CTA ladder
Hero CTA: diagnose current bottleneck
Mid CTA: organize the blocking scene
Final CTA: request free PT diagnosis
Mobile sticky: free PT diagnosis

Do not repeat identical CTA language everywhere.

## 9. Consultation form
Form should collect enough context to make the next conversation better:
- urgent scene/problem
- name/contact
- location
- deadline
- recent difficulty

Preview/prototype pages must not transmit live leads.

## 10. Content Conversion PASS
All must pass:
- clear problem in top 25%
- at least 3 target-specific actual scenes
- at least 4 WHY/CHECK/TRAIN/RECHECK micro-stories or equivalent depth
- observable diagnosis dimensions
- concrete training flow
- progress/recheck logic
- scope boundary with other paths
- no unsupported proof
- deep guide adds genuinely new information
- internal links explain next choice
- contextual CTA after value
- mobile readability maintained

Only after STRUCTURE PASS + CONTENT CONVERSION PASS may a page become a production deploy candidate.
